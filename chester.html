Who we are

A little hill with a big heart

The Chester Bowl Improvement Club began in 1980 and became a Minnesota nonprofit 501(c)(3) in 1982, created to bring quality programming to everyone who loves Chester Park. Since 2008 we've run the park's day-to-day programs — the City of Duluth maintains the grounds, and Chester Bowl provides the people, the lessons, and the fun.

Our mission is simple: healthy living through outdoor recreation, accessible to families of every income. That means a welcoming, safe place to learn to ski and snowboard, affordable equipment rentals, generous scholarships for anyone who needs them, and year-round activities that build confidence in Duluth's kids and teens.

Ski & snowboard programs

A friendly, positive hill right in town — with surprising variety and challenge. Whether you're clicking in for the first time or chasing the terrain park, there's a place for you here.

The Hill

Alpine skiing & snowboarding

A family-friendly hill with affordable prices and a chairlift right in the middle of Duluth. Surprisingly varied terrain for a neighborhood bowl — and the cheapest lift ticket in the country.

Season: mid-Dec through mid-March
Day pass: $15 (18 & under) · $20 (19+)

Group lessons

Free ski & snowboard lessons for all ages, including adults, with any season or day pass. Four ability levels from the bunny hill through freestyle, in friendly 45-minute sessions.

Ages: everyone · Cost: free with a pass
Sign-ups open each fall for the winter season.

Cadet+ leadership

A year-round program built on community, connection & growth. Teens mentor younger campers, take on service projects, earn certifications like CPR/First Aid, and build real job-ready leadership skills.

Ages: entering 7th grade & up · ~10 hrs/month
Cost: $250 (scholarships available)

Terrain park

Girls Shred

A fun, supportive, women-led way for girls, women, and non-binary riders to learn freestyle (terrain park) skiing and snowboarding. Open to anyone who's passed a Level 2 lesson.

Cost: free with registration
Lesson & event dates run January–February.

Fridays

Freestyle Fridays

Low-pressure Friday evening sessions to work on terrain-park skills in a supportive crew — open to skiers, snowboarders, and snowskaters alike. Registration opens 5–6pm in the equipment garage; things kick off at 6:15.

Select Fridays, December through March.

Racing

Alpine Race Series

A low-pressure, fun intro to ski & snowboard racing on Sunday afternoons, with laser timing and an award for every finisher. Age groups from Under-5 to 14–16; complete one Level 2 lesson to join.

When: Sundays in Feb & Mar
Cost: from $10/day · $15 for the season

Growing Up Chester

A brand-new chalet is coming

🏗️ Reopening for the 2026–27 ski season

The Growing Up Chester capital campaign is raising the funds to expand and completely renovate the historic Thom Storm Chalet — preserving Chester's magic for generations to come. The new building will feature:

  • An elevator for full accessibility
  • Expanded gathering spaces for more summer programming
  • Smarter space for busy winter program days
  • Preserved historical character
  • Energy-saving, sustainable materials
