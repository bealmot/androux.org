Terrain Park & Freestyle Fridays

Select Fridays, Dec–Mar · sign up 5–6pm in the equipment garage · drops at 6:15.

Who we are

A little hill with a big heart

The Chester Bowl Improvement Club started in 1980 and became a Minnesota 501(c)(3) nonprofit in 1982. Since 2008 we've run the park's day-to-day programs. The City of Duluth keeps the grounds; Chester Bowl brings the people, the lessons, and the fun.

Our mission is simple: healthy living through outdoor recreation, for families of every income. A welcoming place to learn to ski and snowboard, affordable rentals, generous scholarships, and year-round programs that build confidence in Duluth's kids.

The giant jumps live on.

Those two towers in our logo? Real ski jumps that once made Chester Bowl famous. They're gone from the hill now, but they'll always be our crest, and our heart.
